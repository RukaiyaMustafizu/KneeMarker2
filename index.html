About the KneeMarker Test

The KneeMarker is a lateral flow immunoassay (LFIA) designed for early detection of post-traumatic osteoarthritis (PTOA) in ACL-reconstructed patients. Our test detects three key biomarkers in urine:

CTX-II

C-telopeptide of type II collagen - indicates cartilage breakdown

COMP

Cartilage Oligomeric Matrix Protein - shows cartilage turnover

Neopterin

Differentiates OA from rheumatoid arthritis (RA)

The test uses gold nanoparticle-conjugated antibodies in a lateral flow immunoassay format. When urine is applied, biomarkers (CTX-II, COMP, and Neopterin) bind to specific antibodies, forming visible lines if present.

The test provides qualitative results (positive/negative) with 95% clinical accuracy compared to ELISA standards. No special storage or sample processing is required.

Early detection of PTOA enables:

  • Timely physical therapy interventions
  • Lifestyle modifications to slow progression
  • Potential enrollment in clinical trials
  • Better surgical outcomes when addressed early

Studies show 50% of ACL-reconstructed patients develop PTOA within 12-14 years. Our test detects biochemical changes years before radiographic evidence appears.

KneeMarker Instruction Manual

1

Test Purpose and Principle

The KneeMarker is a rapid urine test that detects early signs of post-traumatic osteoarthritis (PTOA) in patients recovering from ACL injuries by measuring:

  • CTX-II (C-Telopeptide of type II collagen) - cartilage degradation
  • COMP (Cartilage Oligomeric Matrix Protein) - cartilage turnover
  • Neopterin - immune activation (to differentiate from RA)

These markers show biochemical changes before structural damage is visible on imaging.

2

Kit Contents

1× Lateral Flow Test Strip
1× Urine Collection Cup
1× Disposable Dropper
1× Instruction Manual

3

Test Procedure

  1. Collect first-morning urine in the provided cup
  2. Use dropper to apply 3 drops to sample well
  3. Wait 10 minutes for results to develop
  4. Scan test with KneeMarker Pro app or visually interpret lines
